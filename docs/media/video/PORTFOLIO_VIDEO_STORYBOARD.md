## Edit rules

- Use restrained cuts or 220–300 ms crossfades; no bounce, flash, fake terminal typing, or repeated chart reveal.
- Label fixture replay and sealed benchmark footage on first appearance.
- Keep claims identical to README/BENCHMARKS terminology.
- End with documentation paths, not a release/download claim.
